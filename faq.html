Frequently asked questions

Is Wally Street free?

Yes. There are no payments, subscriptions or ads.

Is the money in Practice real?

No. Practice uses simulated prop money (paper money). It starts at $100,000, can never be withdrawn or turned into cash, and real trades are never placed.

Is this financial advice?

No. Wally Street is for learning only. It never tells you what to buy or sell. For real decisions, speak to a qualified, registered adviser.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play as a guest, and your progress is saved in this browser. An account adds a password and, where sync is on, lets you log in with your username and password on any device and carry on where you left off.

I moved the game to a new web address. Where is my progress?

Progress is kept in your browser separately for each web address. On the old address, open ⚙️ → System status, security and backups and tap Download backup. On the new address, open the same screen and tap Restore from file. If you were signed in, log in afterwards with the same username and password.

How do I sync across devices?

Create an account, then log in with the same username and password on your other device. On the website and the download file this works once the operator has switched on the sync server. On the accounts link it works for the person who owns that link. Your progress is encrypted with your password before it is uploaded, so nobody else can read it, which also means a forgotten password cannot be recovered.

Where is my progress saved?

In your browser on this device (encrypted if you have an account). Clearing your browser data deletes it, so download a backup now and then from ⚙️ → System status.

How do hearts work?

You get 5 hearts every day, and all 5 come back at 12:00 AM IST. A wrong answer in a lesson costs one heart. As soon as you lose one, you can earn it back with a 5-question practice (wrong answers there cost nothing), or buy one for 100 Wally coins. Tap the hearts at the top of the screen to do either. History stories, reviews and quizzes never cost hearts.

How many lessons are there?

64 lessons in 16 units: 14 units across 6 levels, a trading strategies unit and an India essentials unit. Every unit ends with a revision that mixes questions from that unit with questions from the units before it, a different mix each time.

What are side quests?

Every unit has a side quest: the 3D bank beside the lesson path. It opens once you finish the unit's lessons. Crack the vault by answering 10 questions from that unit with 3 keys (each wrong answer breaks one; hearts are not used). No mistakes gives 3 stars, one mistake 2 stars and two mistakes 1 star, and you can replay to add stars.

What are the medals?

Only five medals exist in Wally Street, and each is a cross that is very hard to earn. Mastery Cross: finish every lesson in the game, which means all three in each circle (the main lesson, the economics lesson and the revision) and every unit revision. Flawless Cross: finish 30 different lessons in a row without a single mistake. Diamond Cross: get 3 stars on all 16 vaults, then clear the Diamond Vault (30 questions, one key, 15 seconds each). Devotion Cross: reach a 100-day streak. Honour Cross: collect all 20 badges. Each one adds 1,000 Wally coins and stays yours for good. They show in the medal case on your 👤 Profile, next to your name on Learn, and on a picture you can share.

What is Wally Bank?

The Bank tab is where you save, borrow and invest your Wally coins. A savings account earns 1% a week, fixed deposits earn up to 5% if you lock coins away for 4 weeks, a recurring deposit saves a little every day you finish a lesson, and goal pots help you save for a character or outfit. You can also buy mutual funds, whose made-up prices change at random every second, never more than 3% above or below where they started the day. Your net worth is your wallet plus everything saved and invested, minus what you owe. Wally coins have no cash value.

How do loans and the Wally Score work?

The bank offers one random interest rate a day, between 5% and 15%, and you see it before you agree. Borrow 100 coins or more for 3, 7 or 14 days. On the due date the bank takes what you owe from your wallet. If there isn't enough, your wallet goes below zero and you pay a one-off late fee of 10% of what was missing (at least 20 coins). Until you're back above zero, everything you earn pays off the debt and you can't spend coins, but lessons and streaks carry on. Your Wally Score starts at 600: repaying on time adds 25 points and missing a repayment takes away 60. A higher score lets you borrow more at a lower top rate.

How do quiz duels work?

In the Bank tab, open Duels, pick a friend and 5, 10 or 20 questions, then play. Your friend gets the same questions and has 24 hours to start. You're knocked out after 1, 3 or 5 wrong answers. If one of you is knocked out the other wins; if neither is, the faster one wins; if both are, nobody does. The bank pays the winner 75 Wally coins in prize duels: 3 a day, and once a day with each friend. Duels need Friends switched on, on wallystreet.site.

How do I add friends?

Open 👤 Profile and tap Add a friend (or tap Friends on the Learn page), turn friends on, then type a friend's exact username and tap Send request. They see it next time they open Friends and can accept or decline. You can have up to 50 friends. Friends need an account that syncs, on wallystreet.site.

Who can see me on the friends list?

Friends is off until you turn it on, and anyone under 18 is asked to get a parent's or guardian's OK first. Then people who know your exact username can send you a request, and only friends you accept see your username, your character and outfit, level, streak, Wally coins, net worth, lessons done, number of badges, crosses and the last day you played. There is no chat. You can remove a friend or turn friends off at any time, which deletes your card from the server.

What are the three lessons in each circle?

Every circle on the Learn path has three lessons: the main lesson, an economics lesson about the bigger world your money lives in, and a revision of both that also brings back questions from earlier lessons: 2 at the start of the course, rising to 8 by the end, with some from right back at the start. Tap a circle to see all three. Finish all three to open the next circle. Circles you finished before this change stay open, and their economics lessons and revisions are there whenever you like. Economics pays 50 to 80 Wally coins and a revision pays 40.

Which companies can I trade in Practice?

The United States, China and India each have their 50 biggest companies by market value, plus funds that hold many companies at once (the US has 4). Japan was taken out in 5.0 because its prices could only be simulated. Pick a market with the buttons at the top of Practice, then type in the search box to find a company by name or ticker (for example Apple, Tata or Tencent). When live prices are on, US, Indian and Chinese A-share prices can come from the real market (the top of Practice says which markets are live). The rest, including the Hong Kong shares in the China list, start from real prices from the end of October 2026, with simulated moves after that. You trade them with practice money, not Wally coins.

Can I see who a company competes with?

Yes, for the 52 Indian companies, when the game's server has competitor lists switched on. In Practice, open an Indian company and tap 🏢 Competitors under the Buy and Sell boxes. You see up to 8 companies in the same business, biggest first, with what each one does. Tap 📈 Open on one that is in the game to see its chart. You can also ask Wally, for example: Who are Reliance's competitors? The lists come from Upstox, an Indian broker, and are updated about once a week.

What is different in the Google Play app?

The Android app on Google Play is the same game as wallystreet.site. Sign in with the same username and password to carry on where you left off. One difference: in the Google Play app, Ask Wally answers only from its built-in notes, because online answers are switched off there. To delete your account from any browser, see wallystreet.site/delete-account.html.

What is on my profile?

Tap 👤 Profile in the menu at the bottom. It has your character (tap Change to dress up), your stats (Wally coins, hearts, streak, streak freezes, days you practised, lessons and revisions done, level, crosses, badges, vault stars and your Practice account), the medal case with your progress towards each cross, and your friends. Your Wally coins, hearts and streak also stay at the top right of every page. ⚙️ Settings on the Profile page opens your account and settings.

How do I change my character?

Open 👤 Profile in the menu at the bottom, then tap Change under your character. Pick a character (Wally is free; the Fox costs 600 Wally coins, the Eagle 800 and the Lion 1,000), a suit (Zone 1 suits cost 700 coins, Zone 2 300 and Zone 3 100) and a tie (from 100 to 400 coins). Tap anything to try it on first. Things you buy are yours for good, so you can switch between them for free. The new characters only appear in your profile picture, not in lessons.

How does the placement test work?

Pick the level you want to start at. You get 15 questions on the lessons before it, easiest first, and 3 lives just for the test: each wrong answer costs one, and your hearts are not used. Finish with at least one life left and everything before that level is marked done, plus 100 Wally coins. Run out of lives and nothing changes, so you can try again or pick a closer level. Find it on the Learn page or in ⚙️ → Learning.

How do Wally coins and streaks work?

Wally coins are the golden coins with Wally on them (they used to be called XP). You earn them for lessons (50 to 80), history quizzes (80 the first time you finish each one, nothing after that), economics lessons (50 to 80), circle revisions (40), the daily goal (+20), the placement test (+100), the news quiz (+30) and missions (+100). Mistakes reviews give no coins. Your streak grows each day you finish a lesson, and a streak freeze (200 coins, up to 2) protects a day you miss, and 100 coins buy a heart. In Practice, 100 Wally coins turn into $1,000 of practice cash. Wally coins are not real money and have no cash value.

Why can't I trade right now?

Every market in Practice keeps its real hours, Monday to Friday: the US 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM New York time (evening and night in India), closed on US market holidays; China 9:30 AM to 3:00 PM Beijing time; and India 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM IST. When a market is closed, its chart says when it opens next in your own time, and limit and stop-loss orders wait until it opens.

Why are prices different from my broker's?

Where live prices are switched on (the top of Practice says which markets), US, Indian and Chinese A-share prices are real market prices, refreshed every 15 seconds to a minute, so they can be a little behind your broker's. Hong Kong shares, and any market without live prices, start from real closing prices from the date shown in the app, with simulated moves after that, so they won't match live prices.

What do fees and dividends do in Practice?

Each trade costs 0.1% (at least $0.05) so you learn that trading isn't free. Some holdings pay a small simulated dividend each day, based on rough real yields.

Can I see my old Ask Wally chats?

Yes. In Ask, tap 🕘 Chats to see your last 30 chats, newest first. Tap one to read it and carry on where you left off, or tap New chat to start again. Chats are saved only on this device (with an account, locked with your password), so they do not move to your other devices. Delete one with ✕, or all of them with Delete all.

How does the tutor answer?

Wally answers from built-in notes, which work offline. Tap 📋 Available questions to see 70 it always knows, including 20 from the economics lessons. It also knows all 64 economics lessons on the Learn path. On some shared links, Wally can also write fuller answers with an online answer service, if you allow it. Never type passwords, card numbers or ID numbers: the tutor blocks them.

How do I reset or delete things?

To reset Practice, use the reset button at the bottom of the Practice screen. To delete your account, open ⚙️, type your current password and tap Delete my account. You will always be asked to confirm first.

How do classes work?

A teacher makes a class code in ⚙️ → Classroom and shares it. Students join with the code and send back a result code. Nothing goes through a server, so the leaderboard works on trust.

Who can use Wally Street?

People aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before using it.

How do I report a mistake?

Tap 🚩 Report a mistake under any answer, or use the help button → Report a problem.

Who made Wally Street?

Wally Street is made and run by its operator (see Contact).
