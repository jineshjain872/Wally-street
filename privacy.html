Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Version: 1.9

This policy explains what information Wally Street keeps, where it is kept and the choices you have. In short: your progress stays on your device, your account is encrypted with your password, and nothing is sold or shared.

1. WHAT WE KEEP

  • Progress and settings: your Wally coins, Wally Bank, finished lessons, streak, hearts, display name, theme and Practice portfolio. These are saved in your browser's storage on your device.
  • Ask Wally chats: your last 30 chats with the tutor, saved in your browser's storage on this device so you can read them again. With an account they are encrypted with the key made from your password. They are not sent to the cloud copy or the sync server, and they do not move to your other devices.
  • Account (optional): your username and an encrypted copy of your progress. Your password is never stored. It is turned into a key on your device, and only that key can open your data.
  • Cloud copy (accounts link only): when you use the accounts link, an encrypted copy is saved in private storage tied to your sign-in on the platform that hosts the link. Other people cannot see it, and neither can the app's operator.
  • Sync server (website and download file, only if the operator switches it on): an encrypted copy of your progress, kept so you can log in with your username and password on any device. It is encrypted with your password before it leaves your device, so the operator cannot read it. The server also keeps a scrambled key made from your password (never the password itself), the time of your last save, and, for 15 minutes, a count of wrong passwords. The company that hosts the server may log your IP address. Deleting your account deletes this copy too.
  • Friends (only if you turn them on): your username, level, streak, Wally coins, net worth, number of lessons done, number of badges, your crosses, the character and outfit you wear, and the last day you played are kept on the sync server, so the friends you accept can see them. The server also keeps who your friends are and the requests you have sent or received. Turning friends off, or deleting your account, deletes all of it. There is no chat.
  • Quiz duels (only with friends): who challenged whom, the questions, each player's answers, time and result are kept on the sync server for up to 8 days, so both players can see who won.
  • Age check: only whether you passed it, and when. Your age itself is not saved.
  • Choices: when you accepted the Terms and which cookie option you picked.
  • Usage stats (only if you choose "Accept all"): counts such as visits, lessons finished, trades and tutor questions, plus the campaign tags (utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign) of any link you arrived from. They are kept on your device only and never sent anywhere.
  • Daily backups and a short error log: kept on your device to help recover your progress and fix problems.

2. THE TUTOR

On most links the tutor answers from notes built into the app, so your questions never leave your device. On one of the shared links, online answers may be turned on. Your question and the last few chat messages are then sent to the answer service of the platform that hosts the link, to write the reply, under that platform's own privacy policy. On wallystreet.site, online answers are only for adults: players who confirm they are 18 or over before switching them on, or accounts the site's owner lists. For those accounts, the question and the last few chat messages go through the sync server to Google's Gemini API to write the reply, under Google's terms; on Google's free tier Google may keep them to improve its products, and people at Google may read them. Other accounts never send questions anywhere. You can turn online answers off in ⚙️ → System status. The tutor refuses to send messages that look like card numbers, ID numbers or passwords.

3. WHO ELSE IS INVOLVED

  • Google Fonts: your browser downloads the app's fonts from Google, which can see your IP address and browser type.
  • Hosting platform: the shared links are hosted by an outside platform, which has its own privacy policy.
  • News links: they open other websites, which have their own policies.
  • Live prices and competitor lists: where live prices are switched on, the game asks the operator's sync server for the latest prices while Practice is open, and the server gets them from Finnhub (US), Upstox (India) and Tushare (China). Where competitor lists are switched on, tapping 🏢 Competitors on an Indian company (or asking Ask Wally about its competitors) asks the server for that company's list, which the server gets from Upstox. These requests carry only tickers, never your account, progress or chat, but like any website the server sees your device's internet address. Those providers see only the server, not you.

We do not sell your information, show ads or use third-party tracking.

4. COOKIES AND STORAGE

Wally Street does not set cookies. It uses your browser's local storage and IndexedDB, which work in a similar way. Storage that keeps your progress, login and choices is essential, because the app cannot work without it. Usage stats are optional and only kept if you choose "Accept all". You can change your choice at any time in ⚙️ → Cookie settings.

5. CHILDREN

Wally Street is not for children under 13. The app asks your age before you start and does not let anyone under 13 in. Friends stays off until you switch it on, and anyone under 18 is asked to get a parent's or guardian's OK first. There is no chat between players. If you believe a child under 13 has used it, contact us at the same channel through which you got access to Wally Street and we will help remove their data.

6. YOUR CHOICES

  • See what is kept in ⚙️ → System status and ⚙️ → Your usage stats.
  • Download a backup of your data in ⚙️ → System status.
  • Delete your account and its data in ⚙️ → Delete my account.
  • Delete your Ask Wally chats in Ask → 🕘 Chats (one at a time, or all at once).
  • Reset your progress in ⚙️ → Reset. Reset everything also deletes your Ask Wally chats.
  • Clear everything by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

7. HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

Your data stays until you delete it. Sessions that keep you signed in end after 30 days. Daily backups keep only the last 3 days. Usage stats are deleted when you choose "Essential only" or tap "Delete my stats".

8. SECURITY

Accounts are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key made from your password (PBKDF2-SHA-256 with a random salt and 600,000 rounds). No method of storing data is perfectly secure, so use a password you don't use anywhere else.

9. CHANGES

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.

10. CONTACT

Questions or privacy requests: the same channel through which you got access to Wally Street.
