Frequently asked questions

Is Wally Street free?

Yes. There are no payments, subscriptions or ads.

Is the money in Practice real?

No. Practice uses simulated prop money (paper money). It starts at $100,000, can never be withdrawn or turned into cash, and real trades are never placed.

Is this financial advice?

No. Wally Street is for learning only. It never tells you what to buy or sell. For real decisions, speak to a qualified, registered adviser.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play as a guest, and your progress is saved in this browser. An account adds a password and, where sync is on, lets you log in with your username and password on any device and carry on where you left off.

How do I sync across devices?

Create an account, then log in with the same username and password on your other device. On the website and the download file this works once the operator has switched on the sync server. On the accounts link it works for the person who owns that link. Your progress is encrypted with your password before it is uploaded, so nobody else can read it, which also means a forgotten password cannot be recovered.

Where is my progress saved?

In your browser on this device (encrypted if you have an account). Clearing your browser data deletes it, so download a backup now and then from ⚙️ → System status.

How do hearts work?

You get 5 hearts every day, and all 5 come back at 12:00 AM IST. A wrong answer in a lesson costs one heart. As soon as you lose one, you can earn it back with a 5-question practice (wrong answers there cost nothing), or buy one for 100 Wally coins. Tap the hearts at the top of the screen to do either. History stories, reviews and quizzes never cost hearts.

How many lessons are there?

64 lessons in 16 units: 14 units across 6 levels, a trading strategies unit and an India essentials unit. Every unit ends with a revision that mixes questions from that unit with questions from the units before it, a different mix each time.

How do Wally coins and streaks work?

Wally coins are the golden coins with Wally on them (they used to be called XP). You earn them for lessons (50 to 80), the daily goal (+20), the placement test (+100), the news quiz (+30) and missions (+100). Mistakes reviews give no coins. Your streak grows each day you finish a lesson, and a streak freeze (200 coins, up to 2) protects a day you miss, and 100 coins buy a heart. In Practice, 100 Wally coins turn into $1,000 of practice cash. Wally coins are not real money and have no cash value.

Why can't I trade right now?

In Practice the US market is open around the clock, every day, so you can always trade US stocks and funds. The real US market runs 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM Eastern time on weekdays, and the app shows whether it is open right now. China (9:30 AM to 3:00 PM Beijing time), Japan (9:00 AM to 3:30 PM Tokyo time) and India (9:15 AM to 3:30 PM IST) follow their real hours, Monday to Friday. Orders for those markets wait until they open.

Why are prices different from my broker's?

Starting prices are real closing prices from the date shown in the app. After that, the moves are simulated, so they won't match live prices.

What do fees and dividends do in Practice?

Each trade costs 0.1% (at least $0.05) so you learn that trading isn't free. Some holdings pay a small simulated dividend each day, based on rough real yields.

How does the tutor answer?

Wally answers from built-in notes, which work offline. Tap 📋 Available questions to see 50 it always knows. On some shared links, Wally can also write fuller answers with an online answer service, if you allow it. Never type passwords, card numbers or ID numbers: the tutor blocks them.

How do I reset or delete things?

To reset Practice, use the reset button at the bottom of the Practice screen. To delete your account, open ⚙️, type your current password and tap Delete my account. You will always be asked to confirm first.

How do classes work?

A teacher makes a class code in ⚙️ → Classroom and shares it. Students join with the code and send back a result code. Nothing goes through a server, so the leaderboard works on trust.

Who can use Wally Street?

People aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before using it.

How do I report a mistake?

Tap 🚩 Report a mistake under any answer, or use the help button → Report a problem.

Who made Wally Street?

Wally Street is made and run by its operator (see Contact).
