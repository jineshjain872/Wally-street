Delete your Wally Street account

You can delete your Wally Street account and its data yourself, from any web browser. You don't need the app.

Delete it now

  1. Open wallystreet.site in any browser and sign in with your username and password.
  2. Tap ⚙️ at the top right. Under Delete account, type your current password in the password box just above it.
  3. Tap Delete my account, and confirm if you're asked.

Open Wally Street to delete my account

What is deleted

  • Straight away: the encrypted copy of your progress on the sync server, your username, the scrambled key made from your password, your friends card, your friends list and friend requests.
  • Quiz duels you played are deleted within 8 days, as they always are.
  • Progress saved on your own device stays there until you clear it: tap ⚙️ → Reset → Reset everything, clear this site's data in your browser, or uninstall the Wally Street app.

Nothing is kept after deletion, and nothing can be recovered.

Playing without an account?

Then everything is only on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, deletes all of it.

Can't sign in?

Your data is encrypted with your password, so nobody can open it without the password, not even us. If you've forgotten your password and want your account deleted, ask us through the contact email on Wally Street's Google Play page, or the same channel through which you got access to Wally Street. Tell us your username, and we'll delete the account and its data.
